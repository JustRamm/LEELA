# LEELA Driving School 🚗✨

> **"Drive with Confidence"** — A modern, joyful, and high-converting driving academy web application.

---

## 🎨 Visual Identity & Inspiration
LEELA Driving School features a bespoke high-contrast visual identity inspired by modern editorial illustrations:
- **Palette**: Sunny Canary Yellow (`#FFDE17`), Soft Lavender (`#8B6CE6`), Deep Midnight Indigo (`#31276C`), and Golden Amber (`#F5B800`).
- **Hero Perspective**: First-person windshield view into a lilac vehicle driven by a happy learner with in-car holographic GPS navigation against a golden forest horizon.
- **Assets**: Handcrafted scalable SVGs located in `public/brand/` and `public/illustrations/`.
